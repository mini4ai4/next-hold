# Next Hold — a climbing coach on SPECS (Roma's idea, 27.10, msg 14226)

## The one spatial moment
You're on the wall, both hands busy, and you can't see the route from where you hang. The glasses colour every hold you could go to next — green (secure for you, now), yellow (possible), red (risky) — based on **where your body is right now** and **how far you can actually reach**. The next suggested move lights up with the limb to use: "right hand → here".

## Why glasses, not a phone (the contest's three questions)
- **Uses what I'm looking at:** the holds on the wall in front of you, at real size.
- **Puts something in my space:** colour rings sit on the holds themselves, not on a panel.
- **Frees my hands / keeps my eyes up:** a climber can't hold a phone; you look up the wall, not down at a screen.
- Would it be as good on a phone? No: you'd have to let go of the wall.

## How the colour is decided (deterministic, explainable)
For every hold h, from the current position (which holds the two hands and two feet are on):
1. **Grip quality** by hold type: jug 1.0, pinch 0.75, crimp 0.6, pocket 0.55, sloper 0.45.
2. **Reach:** distance from the shoulder of the limb that would move to h, versus the climber's reach (arm length from height × 0.44, entered once). ≤ 80% comfortable, 80–100% a stretch, > 100% out of reach.
3. **Body balance:** a hand move is safer when both feet are on holds and the new hold is above the centre of mass; crossing the body line costs.
4. Score = quality × reach factor × balance factor → **green ≥ 0.7, yellow 0.4–0.7, red < 0.4**. Each hold can show "why": "good jug, but a long reach".
Recomputed every time a hand or foot moves to a new hold.

## Preview stand-ins (the contest rules, preview-limits.md)
- Recognising a real wall isn't possible in the preview → a **virtual climbing wall at real scale** (2.4 m wide, 3 m tall, holds generated by CLAD) placed ~1 m in front, a little below eye level for comfort.
- **Setup:** pinch a hold to mark it and pick its type (real glasses would detect them; the moment of recognition is faked, as the rules suggest).
- **Climbing:** the mouse/pinch is "a hand": pinch a hold to move the selected limb there; the colours recompute. On real SPECS this would come from hand tracking.
- **Out of view:** if the suggested hold is above or beside the view, a small arrow at the edge + a soft chime.

## Done criteria (each checkable in the preview with LEAF)
1. Pinching a hold in setup marks it and shows its type label.
2. In climb mode, every marked hold shows one colour ring (green/yellow/red) that matches the score rule above.
3. Moving the right hand to another hold recomputes all colours within one frame.
4. A hold beyond the climber's reach is always red; a jug within 80% reach with both feet placed is green.
5. The suggested next hold is highlighted with the limb name; if it's outside the view, an edge arrow appears.

## Comfort and safety notes
- Content stays on the wall plane, 0.8–1.2 m away; no floating panels; light colours (additive display: black is invisible).
- Indoor bouldering / top-rope only. Not a substitute for a spotter or instructor; the glasses never say "safe", only "secure grip for you".
