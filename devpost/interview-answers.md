# plan-lens interview — prepared answers (Next Hold)

Purpose: come into the `plan-lens` interview already crisp, so it's ~10 min, not 30.
The skill asks open questions one at a time and refuses "just do it for me" — so these
are answers to *give*, in the learner's own voice, one per turn. They map to the 7
interview topics in `skillpack/skills/plan-lens/SKILL.md`. Don't paste the whole file in;
answer each question as it comes, in these words, and let the agent's follow-ups shape it.

Everything here is checked against `preview-limits.md`: no real-wall recognition, no
hand-tracking, no cross-session memory — all faked with a virtual wall + pinch, built for
the Lens Studio preview on a laptop.

---

## 1. The idea
I climb indoors — bouldering and top-rope. I want glasses that coach me *on the wall*,
in the moment, not an app I study before I get on. When I'm hanging there, the glasses
colour the holds around me by how safe each one is for *me* to move to right now, and
point to the next move.

## 2. The spatial moment
I'm three moves up, both hands on holds, feet planted, and I can't work out where to go
next — the good-looking hold is a long reach and I'm not sure I'll keep my balance. The
glasses light a coloured ring on each hold I could reach: green = secure for me now,
yellow = possible but committing, red = risky or out of reach. The single best next move
glows with the limb to use — "right hand → here". I look *up the wall*, hands stay on the
rock, nothing floats in front of my face.

Why not a phone: I'd have to come off the wall to look at it. The whole value is that my
hands stay on holds and my eyes stay up. It uses all three strengths — it shares my view
(the holds I'm looking at), it draws on the world (rings sit *on* the holds at real size),
and it leaves my body free (no device, eyes up).

## 3. Product or overlay
Overlay — it responds to a real wall and real holds. On real SPECS the glasses would
detect the holds and my limbs. The preview can't recognize a real wall, so the stand-in:
a **virtual bouldering wall at real scale** (2.4 m wide × 3 m tall) spawned ~1 m in front,
a little below eye level, with holds generated by CLAD. I **pinch each hold once to mark
it and pick its type** (jug / crimp / pinch / pocket / sloper) — that fakes the "glasses
recognized the holds" moment. In climb mode, a pinch on a hold means "move the selected
limb to here", which fakes the hand-tracking that would be automatic on the real device.

## 4. Where things live
- I'm looking up and across the wall in front of me, 0.8–1.2 m away.
- **Colour rings:** on each marked hold, flat against the wall plane, sized to the hold
  (~6–10 cm). Not a panel — the colour *is* on the thing it's about.
- **Next-move cue:** a brighter ring + a short text tag ("R hand") floating 5 cm off that
  one hold, on the wall plane, only for the single suggested move.
- **Limb state chip:** a small, dim marker on the 4 holds my hands/feet currently occupy,
  so I can see my own position. On the wall, not on my face.
- **Edge arrow:** if the suggested hold is above/beside the narrow view (37°×32°), a small
  arrow at that edge + a soft chime. Nothing sits behind me or stuck to my face.
- Light colours only (additive display: black is invisible) — bright green/amber/red rings.

## 5. Hands and input
- **Mark a hold (setup):** pinch the hold → a type picker appears (a few big buttons:
  jug/crimp/pinch/pocket/sloper) → pinch the type. Big forgiving targets.
- **Select which limb moves:** four big toggles (LH / RH / LF / RF), pinch to select.
- **Move a limb:** pinch the destination hold → that limb "moves" there → all colours
  recompute. Mouse = one pinching hand in the preview, so every action is a single pinch.
- No two-hand or custom gestures (preview can't show them). No voice as the primary path;
  if we add a "suggest my next move" voice line later, it's paired with a big pinch button
  that does the same thing.

## 6. When things show
- **Setup mode:** only the type picker shows, and only while marking a hold.
- **Climb mode:** rings are always on (that's the point — the safety read), but the
  *text* ("R hand", the "why" label) shows only on the one suggested hold, and fades after
  a couple of seconds or when I move. Everything else recedes.
- **Out of view:** suggested hold off-screen → edge arrow + one soft chime, then quiet.
- The "why" explanation ("good jug, but a long reach") appears only when I pinch-and-hold a
  ring to ask — otherwise it stays out of the way.

## 7. Done criteria (checkable in the preview with LEAF)
1. Pinching a hold in setup marks it and shows its type label; the mark persists.
2. In climb mode every marked hold shows exactly one colour ring (green/yellow/red) that
   matches the scoring rule in `../scoring-reference/` for that body position.
3. Moving the selected limb to another hold recomputes all rings within one frame.
4. A hold beyond the climber's reach is always red; a jug within 80% reach with both feet
   placed and the hold above the centre of mass is green.
5. The single suggested next hold is highlighted with the limb name; if it's outside the
   view, an edge arrow appears at that edge.

## Consent / people
No other people in the scene. Nothing is recorded, no one is identified or followed. The
demo is my own (virtual) wall. — yes.

## Risks to record in the brief
- **Real hold + limb detection** is faked (pinch) in the preview — "logic proven, sensing
  unproven". On real SPECS this needs hold detection + hand tracking.
- **Comfort over a real climb** (glanceability mid-move, tracking dropouts) is "feel
  unproven" — only real glasses can tell.
- Reach is entered once from height (arm reach ≈ height × 0.44); real glasses could measure
  it. Kept as a one-time setup number.

## One-time setup number
Climber reach: default = height × 0.44 (shoulder-to-fingertip of one arm). Entered once in
setup; for the demo, hard-code a 175 cm climber → 77 cm reach.
